Spaceships

Three health, one shield, and a bank of charges nobody can read — not even you.

How it plays

  • Orientation is the notation. Health cards stand upright, the shield lies on its side. Health is a running total on paper; the cards only record where you started.
  • The shield is permanent. It blocks its own value on every single attack, all game, and never wears down. Only a swap ever changes it.
  • Charges are blind. A charge goes from the deck to the table face down and unseen — by the table and by you. Everyone knows the count; nobody knows the total.
  • Say “charge attack” first, or not at all. Declared before the draw, it commits the whole bank — all or nothing. A plain attack is just the one card and never touches your charges.
  • The declaration is binding. Blocked or not, a declared bank is spent. Equal counts as breaking through, so an exact tie deals nothing but still wipes the defender’s charges.
  • Breaking through disarms. The excess comes off health and the defender’s whole bank is destroyed. Their shield is untouched.
  • Cards recycle. Every action draws one. When the deck runs out the discards are reshuffled, so the game only ever ends on health.
