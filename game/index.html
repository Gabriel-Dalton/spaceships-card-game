  • Orientation is the notation. Health cards stand upright, the shield lies on its side. Health itself lives on the paper, not the table.
  • The shield is permanent. It blocks its own value on every attack, all game. Only a swap ever changes it.
  • Charges are blind. Face down from the deck, unseen by the table and by their owner. Everyone knows the count; nobody knows the total.
  • Say “charge attack” before you draw, or not at all. It commits the whole bank — all or nothing — and it is binding whether it lands or is blocked. A plain attack is one card and never touches your charges.
  • Breaking through disarms. Excess damage comes off health and the defender’s entire bank is destroyed. Their shield is untouched. Equal counts as breaking through.
  • Click a seat to aim. A plain attack costs you nothing, which makes it the cheap way to wipe somebody else’s stockpile.
